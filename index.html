Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for OfferPanda. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data in strict compliance with the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide you with the best possible experience on the OfferPanda app. This includes basic account information and technical data required for the app's operation.

2. Location Services and "Walk-In Deals"

To provide you with our exclusive "Walk-In Deals" functionality, the OfferPanda application explicitly requires the collection of precise location data. This includes background location data to alert you of relevant offers when you are near participating stores, even if the app is not actively open on your screen. You have full control over these permissions and can disable them at any time via your device settings.

3. Data Storage & Financial Transactions

Your security is paramount. No financial data is stored on our servers. All transactions are handled securely via Google Play Billing. Any payment processing is routed through encrypted, industry-standard gateways, ensuring that your sensitive payment information remains confidential.

4. Your Rights Under the PDPA

In accordance with the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act, you have the right to access, rectify, and request the deletion of your personal data. For any privacy-related inquiries, please contact our support team.
