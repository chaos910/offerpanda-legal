Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 9, 2026
Last Updated: May 9, 2026

Welcome to OfferPanda ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the OfferPanda mobile application (the "App").

This policy applies to all users of the App, including everyday consumers ("Users") and registered businesses ("Merchants"). By using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to you. We gather this data in three ways:

A. Information You Provide to Us Directly

  • Account Registration Data: When you sign up, we collect your email address, password (stored securely as a cryptographic hash), and a user-generated profile name.
  • Merchant Profile Data: If you register as a Store Owner, we collect your business name, physical address, contact numbers, and any images/logos you upload. Note: Merchant profile data is intended to be publicly visible on the App.
  • Communication Data: If you contact our support team, we collect your email address and the contents of your message.

B. Information We Collect Automatically

  • Location Information: With your explicit permission, we collect your device’s precise geolocation data (GPS) to show you relevant, nearby deals and calculate distances to stores. This includes background location data to alert you of relevant offers when you are near participating stores, even if the app is not actively open on your screen. If you deny this permission, we may use your IP address to determine your general location (e.g., city-level) to provide basic functionality.
  • Device Information: We collect hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: We track how you interact with the App. This includes the time you log in, the offers you view, the deals you save to your wallet, and the stores you follow.
  • Push Notification Tokens: We generate a unique device token to send you real-time alerts regarding expiring deals or stores you follow.

C. Information from Third Parties

  • Payment & Subscription Data: If you purchase a Premium Subscription, your payment is processed directly by Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchases. OfferPanda does not collect or store your credit card numbers. We only receive a secure digital receipt (a purchase token) via RevenueCat to verify your active subscription status.

2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use your data to operate, improve, and secure the OfferPanda platform. Your information is used to:

  • Provide Core Services: To create your account, display local deals, and facilitate the saving/claiming of offers.
  • Personalize the Experience: To rank and recommend deals based on your location and past engagement (e.g., our Bayesian Rating system and distance algorithms).
  • Process Subscriptions: To unlock Premium features for Users and Merchants upon successful payment verification.
  • Provide Analytics to Merchants: We provide Store Owners with aggregated, anonymized data (e.g., "150 people viewed this offer, and 20 saved it"). We absolutely never share a User's personal email address or exact identity with Store Owners.
  • Communicate with You: To send administrative emails, policy updates, and push notifications (which you can opt out of at any time).

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

We only share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers (Our Infrastructure): We share data with trusted third-party companies that host our database, process our notifications, and manage our subscriptions. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Supabase: For secure cloud database hosting and user authentication.
  • RevenueCat: For verifying App Store/Play Store subscriptions.
  • Google Firebase/GCP: For crash reporting, analytics, and delivering push notifications.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose your data if required by law, subpoena, or other legal processes, or to protect the rights, property, and safety of OfferPanda, our users, or the public.
  • Business Transfers: If OfferPanda is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your data may be transferred as part of that business transaction.

4. Data Security and Retention

Security Measures: We implement robust, industry-standard security protocols. Our backend database utilizes Row Level Security (RLS) to ensure that Users and Merchants can only access data they are authorized to view. All data transmitted between your app and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS.

Data Retention: We retain your personal data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently erased from our active databases within 30 days, except for anonymized telemetry data or information we are legally required to keep for tax/accounting purposes.

5. International Data Transfers

OfferPanda is operated in Sri Lanka; however, the third-party cloud infrastructure we use (such as Supabase and Google) operates on global servers. By using the App, you acknowledge that your data may be transferred to, stored, and processed in facilities located outside of Sri Lanka. We ensure our service providers adhere to internationally recognized data protection standards.

6. Your Rights (PDPA Compliance)

Under the Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 of Sri Lanka, and in compliance with global App Store guidelines, you retain full control over your data:

  • The Right to Access & Rectification: You can view and update your profile information directly within the App's settings.
  • The Right to Erasure (Account Deletion): You have the right to be forgotten. You can instantly delete your account and all associated personal data by navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account inside the App.
  • The Right to Withdraw Consent: You can revoke our access to your device's location or push notification permissions at any time through your mobile device's native OS settings.

7. Children's Privacy

OfferPanda is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor without parental consent, we will take immediate steps to delete that information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, if the changes are significant, by sending an in-app notification.
